# **About Me**

Hi, I am **{{ site.author.name }}** :wave:

I am a Full Stack Developer with 5 years of experience delivering backend services, APIs, integrations, and production applications across financial-market, banking, and enterprise systems. My primary backend languages are Golang and PHP, with additional experience in OutSystems and Python.

At the Indonesia Stock Exchange (IDX), I develop Golang backend features for sustainability, sharia-investment, and risk-monitoring systems. My work covers RESTful APIs, microservices, event-driven communication, Apache Kafka, PostgreSQL, Redis, Kubernetes, Jenkins CI/CD, Podman, SonarQube, and Nexus Repository. I also support production operations as a first-level on-call contact, including error analysis and issue resolution.

Previously, at Melkhior Teknologi, I developed and maintained HRIS, product-management, voucher-management, TV-content, and core-banking integrations using PHP, Laravel, CodeIgniter, MySQL, and related web technologies. I worked directly with clients from requirements analysis through implementation, demonstration, release, and production support.
